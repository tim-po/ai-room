## Learning evidence: what it can and cannot justify

Reopened original [Roediger & Karpicke 2006 paper](https://learninglab.psych.purdue.edu/downloads/2006/2006_Roediger_Karpicke_PsychSci.pdf). Controlled prose-recall experiments support delayed memory benefit from retrieval under their tested conditions; restudy did better immediately. This justifies D06 as a learning hypothesis, not equating immediate success or memorized answers with applied AI skill. Acceptance still requires approved questions and explanations, which this fixture lacks.

Reopened [Cepeda et al. 2008 author manuscript](https://escholarship.org/content/qt0kp5q19x/qt0kp5q19x_noSplash_aedacd613d87c6d68878ac7ba09fdf0b.pdf). Gap/retention-delay findings motivate optional later review; they do not justify universal daily pressure, a fixed “optimal” interval or product D7 uplift. D07 first needs correct saved-work continuation. Feedback evidence/caveat remains S03 in original register, based on the author institution abstract; no new full-text access claimed.

Strength: experimental support for specific learning mechanisms; indirect transfer to AI Room projects; **no causal evidence for this palette, map geometry, onboarding conversion or product retention**. Screenshots, simulation and four synthetic completions cannot establish human task time, comprehension, activation rates or return uplift.

## Measurement decisions and measurable hypotheses

Adopt the existing research contract and manager 004 elapsed-window decision, with learning-002 clarification: artifact activation is **explicit nonempty result submission**, not any draft save. Draft save measures continuity. A changed meaningful submission/revision or completed reviewed challenge may qualify; unchanged resaves, GET/login/video polling, and bare completion toggles do not. Do not treat saved arbitrary text as proven learning or competence. Report artifact and reviewed-challenge outcomes separately.

- H1: short skippable onboarding → suitable small task improves **7-day activation**. Denominator: first-time exposed eligible nonfixture learners with full 168h follow-up, including skippers. Numerator: first qualifying result in [exposure, exposure+168h). Guardrails: blocked browsing, wrong recommendations, failed saves. Reject hypothesis if extra steps reduce useful results. Current four fixtures validate plumbing only.
- H2: correct latest-work continuation improves **successful restore / explicit resume requests**, with D1/D7 meaningful return secondary. Wrong-work restores are a guardrail and currently observed failure. No click-only success claim.
- H3: optional reviewed retrieval/explanation improves **delayed performance on independently reviewed equivalent tasks**, with return and opt-out secondary. Requires reviewed inventory; repeated exposed questions and answer copying cannot show transfer.

D1 window [t0+24h,t0+48h); D7 [t0+168h,t0+192h), t0 first qualifying meaningful activity. Include only cohorts with complete 48h/192h follow-up. Zero denominator null. Report raw numerator/denominator, UTC windows, cohort/fixture exclusions and uncertainty. Navigation funnel uses 24h mature observation, reports skip separately; no progress is not proven abandonment. Time-to-first-result uses median/p75 among converters plus total eligible and conversion rates; wall-clock time includes interruption.

Implement minimal allowlisted versioned exposure/step/skip/completion, substantive result, successful resume and genuine feedback-view events with backend ownership. Distinguish server-delivered onboarding from visible exposure; action rows alone are not exposure. No raw answer/draft/session/email in events. Reconcile synthetic HTTP actions with stored rows, retries, unchanged writes and boundary timestamps before reporting any rate. Preserve legacy metrics under their original names. Do not invent baseline, sample size or target uplift before real eligible data exists.
